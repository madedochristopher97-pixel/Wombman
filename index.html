  • Relieves pelvic tension and supports natural cyclical flow
  • Nurtures deep nervous system relaxation and stress relief
  • Infused with anti-inflammatory, antioxidant-rich botanicals
  • 100% natural, cruelty-free, and chemical-free formulation
1. Bring 4–6 cups of filtered water to a gentle boil.
2. Add 2–3 tablespoons of Wombman blend, cover, and steep for 10 minutes.
3. Place vessel securely under a perforated steam stool or chair.
4. Wrap a warm blanket around your waist and relax over the gentle steam for 15–20 minutes.
Steam warmth opens skin pores, increases local circulation, and delivers volatile essential oils directly to pelvic tissues, releasing muscular tightness and inviting peaceful stillness.
Ideal for 3–5 days prior to your cycle start, after menstruation finishes, or whenever you seek intentional self-care and grounding. Avoid steaming during active menstrual bleeding or pregnancy.
